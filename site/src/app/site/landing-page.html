01 / Mission

Learning in your own language can change everything.

Gemma 3 4B makes things up when it answers in Yorùbá. We ground its answers in a verified corpus of 15,791 rows and show the sources under every answer.

  • Local languages

    Start from the language students already speak. This version supports Yorùbá and English.

  • Learning without a connection

    The same backend can run on one machine in a school. Internet is needed only once, to download the models and the search index.

  • Research

    We also review how African languages are documented in open models. See the language map.

02 / The tutor

A tutor that answers from verified data.

Ask in Yorùbá or English. The tutor searches the corpus, gives the best matches to Gemma, and writes the main explanation in Yorùbá with key terms in English. Turn on compare mode to see plain Gemma and Gemma + RAG answer the same question side by side.

07 / Transparency

Information matters. So does context.

The goal of the research is to find learning and collaboration opportunities without oversimplifying the reality of each community.

  1. 01

    No evidence is not the same as not existing.

    A language missing from the reviewed models may be covered by other resources. The real quality of answers was not measured.

  2. 02

    Connectivity is not educational capacity.

    The share of people using the internet does not measure access to school materials or knowledge of other languages.

  3. 03

    Result labels are not tested ability.

    They show what a desk review found in open catalogues. No model was tested on these languages.

  4. 04

    Communities first.

    Tools must be validated with teachers, organizations and speakers of each language variety.
