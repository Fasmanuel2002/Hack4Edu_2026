How this base was built

Research closed on {{ analysisDate }}. It covers {{ summary.africanCountries }} African countries and {{ summary.territories }} separate territory. The unit of analysis is the country–language pair: {{ summary.languageCountryRows }} records and {{ summary.uniqueLanguages }} unique languages.

The dataset shows which corpora and models document a language, not tested proficiency. No inference or comprehension tests were run in local languages, so coverage says nothing about quality, safety or how well a tutor would serve real speakers.

Organizations list

The CRM starts with {{ seedCount }} organizations: 6 recovered from the original CRM (Nigeria), 55 from documentary research on 2026-11-01, and {{ seedCount - 61 }} added by the public contact research of the same day. The earlier list of 64 organizations could not be fully recovered, so this is a separate recovered and researched set, not that directory.

The Contacts view lists {{ allContacts().length }} contact routes from that research, collected from the organizations' own pages and public directories (no LinkedIn). Most are organization-level; a few named leaders appear exactly as their organization's page lists them. AFECO (Nigeria) has its identity pending verification. {{ contactNotice }}
